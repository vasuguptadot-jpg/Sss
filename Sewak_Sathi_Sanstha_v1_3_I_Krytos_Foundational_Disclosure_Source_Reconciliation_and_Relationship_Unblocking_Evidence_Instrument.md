## 0. THE OPERATING RULES OF THIS INSTRUMENT

### 0.1 The single question

**[P]** v1.3-H established that **the analytical method is complete and the foundational subject is not established.** v1.3-I therefore does **no new analysis of the relationship.** It performs one operation:

> **Take the evidence state recorded by v1.3-H, test it against everything now supplied, and report the delta — including a delta of zero.**

**[P] Rule I-1 (The Zero Delta Is A Result).** A finding that nothing changed **is** a finding, and is recorded with the same rigour as a finding that something changed. **This instrument does not compensate for an empty evidence intake by analysing harder.**

### 0.2 What may not be used as evidence

**[P] Rule I-2 (Evidence Hierarchy).** The mandate's three prohibitions are adopted verbatim as operating rules:

**[P] Rule I-3 (The Absolute Rule, itemised).** No missing field may be filled by any of the following, **unless the evidence explicitly establishes the fact**:

1. **inference** · 2. **probability** · 3. **historical assumption** · 4. **founder identity** · 5. **repeated architectural language** · 6. **repository ownership** · 7. **domain ownership** · 8. **brand usage** · 9. **personal recollection**

**Each of the nine is separately searched for and separately reported at Part XXIII.**

**[P] Rule I-4 (`NOT ESTABLISHED`).** Where evidence is insufficient, the field reads **`NOT ESTABLISHED`**. **`NOT ESTABLISHED` is not `absent`, not `nonexistent`, not `no`, and not `unlikely`.** It records the state of the evidence and makes no claim about the state of the world. **Krytos may exist in law. This instrument cannot say so, and cannot say otherwise.**
