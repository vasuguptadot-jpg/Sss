# PART IX — DOCUMENT CONTENT INTAKE AND CLAIM-LEVEL PROVENANCE

## IX.0 Method

> **Rule K-48 (Extract Before Interpreting).** *"Do not immediately interpret the entire document as authoritative."* **A received document is first reduced to explicit extracts in nine categories, and each extract is then classified individually. The document as a whole is never classified, never adopted, and never allowed to confer status on a claim it does not contain.**

## IX.1 The nine extraction categories — EX-1 … EX-9

**[P]** *"First extract … Then classify each extracted claim."* **Only what the document states explicitly is extracted. Nothing is extracted by implication, inference, structural resemblance, repeated language or architectural fit.**

| ID | **Category** | **What is extracted** | **What is never extracted** | **The architectural question it feeds** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EX-1** | **Explicit claims** | Any sentence asserting a state of affairs | Anything the document *implies*, *presupposes* or *assumes* | All |
| **EX-2** | **Explicit dates** | Any stated date, with what it is the date *of* | A date inferred from a version number, a filename, a sequence or a context | **SA-03** effective date; **DR-11** |
| **EX-3** | **Explicit actors** | Any named person or body, with the capacity stated *in the document* | A capacity inferred from a title, a signature block's absence, or a role name | **SA-27** authority; **A-05** |
