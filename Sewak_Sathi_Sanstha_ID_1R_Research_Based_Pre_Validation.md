## II.3 The nine classifications

**[P]** Mandate §6, step 7. **These nine are the only permitted classifications. They are not interchangeable, and the test for each is stated so that a finding cannot be upgraded into a stronger one.**

| Classification | **The test — a finding may carry this classification only if** | **What it does not mean** |
|---|---|---|
| ### **`SUPPORTED BY PRIMARY SOURCE`** | **A Tier 1 source was located, its text or official summary was read, and the proposition is what the source says** | **That the proposition applies to Sewak Sathi. **Application is fields 8 and 13** |
| ### **`SUPPORTED BY SECONDARY SOURCE ONLY`** | **Only Tier 2 or Tier 3 material was located, and no Tier 1 source was found for the proposition** | **That the proposition is wrong. **It means it is unverified against the primary text** |
| ### **`PARTIALLY SUPPORTED`** | **The source supports part of the proposition and is silent or unclear on the rest** | **That the unsupported part is false** |
| ### **`ENTITY-DEPENDENT`** | **The answer differs according to which legal form is chosen, and no form has been chosen** | **That the answer is unknowable. **Part VIII shows what each candidate form would produce** |
| ### **`FACT-DEPENDENT`** | **The answer turns on a fact that no source can supply — how people actually work, what has actually happened, who holds what** | **That research failed. **It means research was the wrong instrument for that part** |
| ### **`AMBIGUOUS`** | **The source is capable of more than one reading and this instrument will not choose between them** | **That the ambiguity is resolvable by more research. **Often it is resolvable only by interpretation** |
| ### **`SOURCE CONFLICT`** | **Two or more sources of comparable standing say different things** | **That one is wrong. **Part XVII records both, their dates, the hierarchy where established, and whether the conflict can be reconciled** |
| ### **`NOT ESTABLISHED`** | **No source sufficient to the proposition was located in this research** | **That the proposition is false, or that no source exists anywhere. **It means this research did not find one** |
| ### **`PROFESSIONAL VALIDATION REQUIRED`** | **The proposition cannot responsibly be settled by public research at all — it needs facts, judgement, interpretation, or a filing** | **That the research was wasted. **`ID-1` exists precisely to route these questions** |
